SCHOOL OF CHILD SAFEGUARDING (SCS) — PROTOCOL DOCUMENT
Title: Suicidal Ideation and Crisis Intervention Protocol
Category: Suicidal Ideation
Version: 5.0 | Last Updated: March 2025
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — Crisis-Trained Social Workers Only

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1: GUIDING PRINCIPLES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every expression of suicidal ideation must be taken seriously. There is no "cry wolf" in suicide risk — all disclosures are treated as genuine until assessed otherwise.

The helper's role in a crisis is to:
1. Maintain calm and connection.
2. Assess risk level accurately.
3. Activate appropriate support systems.
4. Ensure the youth is never left alone in acute crisis.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2: IMMEDIATE RISK INDICATORS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Social media signals triggering CRITICAL classification:
- Explicit statements of intent to die or end one's life
- Posts referencing specific methods (overdose, jumping, cutting)
- Giving away possessions or posting "goodbye" messages
- References to final acts (writing letters, settling affairs)
- Sudden calmness after period of distress (may indicate decision has been made)

Risk stratification using the Suicide Assessment Framework (SAF):
- Ideation only (no plan, no intent): Moderate risk
- Ideation + plan (method identified): High risk
- Ideation + plan + means (method accessible): Critical risk
- Ideation + plan + means + timeline: EMERGENCY — call 995

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3: CRISIS CALL PROTOCOL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When contacting a youth in crisis:

Step 1 — Open with care, not alarm:
"I saw your post and I wanted to check in on you. I care about how you're doing."

Step 2 — Ask directly about suicide:
Research shows asking directly does NOT increase risk. Ask:
"Are you having thoughts of killing yourself?"

Step 3 — If yes, assess plan and means:
"Do you have a plan for how you would do it?"
"Do you have access to [method]?"
"When are you thinking of doing this?"

Step 4 — If imminent danger:
- Do not end the call.
- Have a colleague call 995 from a separate phone.
- Keep youth talking until emergency services arrive.
- Try to obtain physical location from youth.

Step 5 — If non-imminent (ideation without plan):
- Validate their pain: "I hear how much you're hurting."
- Safety plan: "Can we make a plan together for tonight?"
- Identify someone to be with them: "Is there someone who can stay with you?"
- Give crisis numbers: Samaritans of Singapore 1-767 (24/7), IMH Emergency 6389 2222

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4: MANDATORY NOTIFICATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Parents/Guardians
- Notify immediately in all cases of suicidal ideation (regardless of youth's age or consent).
- Exception: if parents are the source of risk (abuse, rejection of identity). In this case, contact alternative guardian or school.
- Provide parents with Samaritans of Singapore parent guide.

4.2 School
- Notify Student Welfare Officer (SWO) or Principal on same business day.
- Do not share case details beyond what is necessary for safety.
- School must activate their own crisis protocol.

4.3 SCS Supervisor
- Notify supervisor within 1 hour of identifying critical case.
- Complete Critical Incident Report (CIR) within 24 hours.

4.4 SPF / Welfare Check
- Request SPF welfare check if:
  (a) Youth is uncontactable after expressing imminent intent
  (b) Physical location is known and risk is critical
  (c) Youth has explicitly refused help and risk is assessed as imminent

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: HOSPITAL AND CLINICAL REFERRAL PATHWAY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 A&E (Emergency Department)
- If youth has already attempted self-harm or suicide, accompany to A&E if possible.
- Alert the A&E team to suicidal context before arrival.
- Do not leave youth unattended while waiting.

5.2 IMH (Institute of Mental Health)
- For outpatient psychiatric assessment: refer via polyclinic or GP.
- For urgent inpatient admission: go directly to IMH A&E at 10 Buangkok View.
- IMH emergency line: 6389 2222

5.3 Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS) Subsidies
- Youth from lower-income families may be eligible for CHAS subsidies at CHAS GPs.
- Discuss financial concerns with family before referral to avoid barriers.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: RECURRING SUICIDAL IDEATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Cases with 2 or more documented instances of suicidal ideation:
- Mandatory clinical review after 2nd instance.
- Intensive case management (minimum weekly contact) after 3rd instance.
- SCS case conference with clinical team and school after 4th instance.
- Consider residential or day programme placement.
- Review effectiveness of current interventions — change approach if no improvement.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: POST-CRISIS FOLLOW-UP
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

After a crisis event:
- Day 1: Confirm youth's safety. Check in with family.
- Day 3: Brief wellbeing check-in.
- Day 7: Full session. Review safety plan. Update risk assessment.
- Day 14: Check in with school.
- Month 1: Case review with supervisor.

If youth was hospitalised:
- Liaise with hospital social worker for discharge planning.
- Arrange aftercare services before discharge.
- Update case management system with hospitalisation record.
