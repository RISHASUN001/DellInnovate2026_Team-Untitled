## SCS PROTOCOLS: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC INTERVENTION GUIDELINES

=== SELF-HARM IDEATION ===

DEFINITION: Cases flagged for language or behaviour patterns associated with self-harm or suicidal ideation. These are the highest-priority cases and require immediate, trauma-informed response.

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- Risk score 4 or 5: escalate within 2 hours if no response.
- Any explicit mention of method, timing, or farewell messages: immediate escalation regardless of score.
- Youth under 14 with any score ≥ 3: escalate immediately.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Do not send generic automated messages. Use personalised, warm language.
2. Acknowledge feelings without minimising: "That sounds really painful."
3. Ask open-ended check-in questions: "How are you doing today?"
4. Do not ask directly about methods in the first message.
5. Offer one concrete next step: "There are people here who want to support you, whenever you're ready."
6. If no response within 3 hours for risk 5 cases, escalate to team lead immediately.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Review AI signals and verify escalation threshold
- Attempt initial outreach via platform DM
- Document response or non-response within 2 hours
- Escalation considered and decision recorded
- Refer to crisis support line if unresponsive
- Case closed or transferred to clinical team

CRISIS RESOURCES:
- Samaritans of Singapore (SOS): 1800-221-4444 (24/7)
- IMH Emergency: 6389 2222
- Community Health Assessment Team (CHAT): 6493 6500

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 5: outreach within 2 hours, follow-up at 24h, 48h, 72h.
- Risk 4: outreach within 6 hours, follow-up at 48h, 72h, 1 week.

TRAUMA-INFORMED PRINCIPLES:
- Avoid language that implies blame ("what were you thinking?")
- Validate emotions before offering solutions
- Do not share resources as the only response — connect first
- Use "I" statements: "I noticed…", "I was thinking of you…"

=== CYBERBULLYING ===

DEFINITION: Systematic online harassment including targeted hate, doxxing attempts, coordinated campaigns, or sustained negative interactions from one or more accounts.

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- Doxxing or physical threat: immediate escalation regardless of score.
- Youth has completely stopped posting after incident: high concern, escalate if score ≥ 4.
- Coordinated campaigns (3+ accounts): escalate to team lead.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Assess whether harassment is ongoing or completed.
2. Document and report perpetrator accounts to the platform via formal report process.
3. Outreach to the targeted youth: focus on their safety and wellbeing, not the perpetrators.
4. Avoid language that centres perpetrators; keep focus on the youth's experience.
5. Do not contact perpetrators.

SCHOOL COORDINATION:
- If the perpetrators are known school peers, contact SCS School Liaison Officer.
- Coordinate with school counsellor for in-person support.
- Do not share case details with the school without informed consent from youth/guardian.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Identify perpetrator accounts for reporting
- Draft outreach message (non-confrontational)
- Outreach attempt logged
- Report perpetrator accounts to platform
- Coordinate with school liaison if school-based
- Follow-up scheduled

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 4: outreach within 6h, follow-up at 48h, 1 week.
- Risk 3: outreach within 24h, follow-up at 3 days, 2 weeks.

=== BULLYING ===

DEFINITION: Repeated, intentional harmful behaviour directed at a youth, including social exclusion, verbal abuse, and threat-based harassment. Distinguished from cyberbullying by primarily offline or direct social media targeting.

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- Physical threats (mentions of physical harm): escalate immediately.
- Youth expresses fear for safety: escalate within 24h.
- Score ≥ 4 with no helper response in 72h: auto-escalation flag.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Validate the youth's experience: "That sounds really unfair, and it's not okay."
2. Assess immediate safety: "Are you currently safe?"
3. Do not confront the perpetrator directly or advise the youth to confront them.
4. Explore support network: "Is there a teacher or adult you trust?"
5. For physical threats: escalate to school or relevant authorities.

SCHOOL COORDINATION:
- Physical threats require immediate school notification.
- Social exclusion cases: coordinate after youth consent is obtained.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Review signals: frequency and severity
- Outreach attempted
- Response received from youth
- School coordination required?
- Follow-up scheduled
- Escalation considered

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 4: outreach within 6h, follow-up at 48h, 1 week.
- Risk 3: outreach within 24h, follow-up at 3 days, 2 weeks.
- Risk 2: outreach within 48h, follow-up at 1 week, 1 month.

=== FAMILY CONFLICT ===

DEFINITION: Cases where the youth reports or signals domestic tension, conflict with family members, or unsafe home conditions. May co-occur with other categories (Self-Harm, Isolation).

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- Youth mentions feeling unsafe at home: immediate escalation.
- Mentions of running away or being told to leave: escalate within 24h.
- Score ≥ 4: escalate regardless of other signals.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Use trauma-informed, non-judgmental language.
2. Do not frame family conflict as the youth's fault.
3. Assess safety first: "Right now, do you feel safe where you are?"
4. Explore trusted adults: "Is there a relative, teacher, or friend's parents you trust?"
5. Do not advise the youth to confront family members.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Assess immediate safety risk
- Outreach attempted
- Family systems referral considered
- Safe housing options reviewed if needed
- Follow-up scheduled
- Case closed or escalated

REFERRAL OPTIONS:
- Family Service Centres (FSC): refer if parental consent obtainable
- REACH (Response, Early Intervention and Assessment in Community Mental Health)
- If immediate danger: Police (999) or Crisis Shelter referral

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 5: outreach within 2h, follow-up at 24h, 48h.
- Risk 4: outreach within 6h, follow-up at 48h, 1 week.
- Risk 3: outreach within 24h, follow-up at 3 days, 2 weeks.

=== LONELINESS / ISOLATION ===

DEFINITION: Cases characterised by social withdrawal, reduced peer interaction, expressions of loneliness, or passive digital consumption replacing social engagement.

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- If isolation is accompanied by distress language scoring ≥ 4: treat as Self-Harm risk.
- Zero peer interactions for 21+ days combined with hopeless language: escalate.
- Score ≥ 4: treat with Self-Harm Ideation protocol overlay.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Use warm, low-pressure outreach: avoid implying there's something wrong with the youth.
2. Focus on connection, not problem-solving in first contact.
3. Suggest low-stakes activities or groups (online or in-person).
4. Do not immediately push resources — build relationship first.
5. Check in with frequency rather than intensity.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Assess degree of social withdrawal
- Outreach attempted (warm, low-pressure)
- Youth group or social activity referral considered
- Response received
- Follow-up scheduled

REFERRAL OPTIONS:
- Youth.sg activities directory
- SCS social skills groups (check availability)
- School counsellor for peer-connect programmes

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 3: outreach within 24h, follow-up at 3 days, 1 week.
- Risk 2: outreach within 48h, follow-up at 1 week, 2 weeks.

=== ACADEMIC STRESS ===

DEFINITION: Cases flagged for stress language specifically tied to academic performance, examinations, or educational pressure. Typically lower urgency unless combined with hopelessness or self-worth language.

ESCALATION THRESHOLD:
- Academic stress combined with hopeless or worthless language: treat as Self-Harm risk.
- Score ≥ 3: outreach within 24h.
- "Giving up" language: check for deeper ideation before outreach.

FIRST RESPONSE PROTOCOL:
1. Validate stress: "Exam periods are really tough, and it makes sense you're feeling the pressure."
2. Normalise difficulty without minimising: "A lot of young people feel this way."
3. Share practical resources: counselling, study support, tutoring.
4. Check for underlying family or social stressors.
5. Monitor for escalation to more serious categories.

MANDATORY CHECKLIST STEPS:
- Assess stress severity
- Outreach attempted if risk ≥ 3
- Resources shared (counselling, study support)
- Follow-up if escalation observed
- Case closed

RESOURCES:
- MOE School Counselling
- ComCare Short Term Assistance for tutoring support
- Youth mental health helplines

FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:
- Risk 3: outreach within 24h, follow-up at 3 days.
- Risk 2: outreach within 48h, follow-up at 1 week.
- Risk 1: monitor, outreach within 1 week if signals persist.

=== CROSS-CATEGORY PRINCIPLES ===

WHEN IN DOUBT: Escalate. It is always safer to escalate unnecessarily than to underestimate risk.

CO-OCCURRING CATEGORIES: A case may span multiple categories (e.g., Family Conflict + Self-Harm). Always apply the higher-urgency protocol.

PRIVACY: Never share personal information about the youth with anyone outside the SCS team without explicit guardian or legal consent.

DOCUMENTATION: All outreach attempts, responses, decisions, and escalations must be logged in the checklist with a timestamped comment. This creates the audit trail needed for safeguarding compliance.

YOUTH AUTONOMY: Youth above 14 have a right to privacy. Do not contact family without the youth's knowledge unless there is an immediate safety concern.
